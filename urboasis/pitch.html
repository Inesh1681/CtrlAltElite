Civic tech · Bengaluru · SDG 11

Getting a complaint filed is easy. Getting it fixed is the hard part.

UrbOasis is the follow-through layer for civic complaints. It routes each problem to the right department, writes the complaint, and tells citizens and housing societies exactly when and how to follow up, escalate and file an RTI.

Report it to the right desk, then follow up on schedule until it’s fixed.

Problem analysis

Civic complaints don’t fail at reporting. They fail after it.

Follow a pothole from the moment someone notices it. Tap each stage to see where it breaks down, and what UrbOasis does about it.

Fragmented ownership

Roads, garbage and streetlights sit with GBA, water and sewage with BWSSB, power with BESCOM, signals with Traffic Police. BBMP’s 2025 split into GBA and five city corporations added more confusion.

No follow-through

Helplines take complaints, but nobody tells the citizen when a complaint is overdue, who to escalate to, or what to write. Most people give up after one call.

No shared evidence

Every complaint lives alone. Nobody can show that a ward’s potholes take three weeks, so there’s no pressure to improve.

Innovation

The first tool that manages what happens after you complain

Existing channels are built to receive complaints. UrbOasis is built to get them resolved: a rules-based escalation engine that knows each department’s chain and the citizen’s legal tools.

Independent, not impersonating

Clearly states that it is not a government site. Citizens file through official channels, so complaints stay on the official record.

Built

Data minimisation

No accounts, no tracking and no central database today. Personal details stay on the device and are used only to sign letters.

Grounded in existing law

The pitch

Bengaluru doesn’t need another place to complain. It needs complaints that get fixed.

UrbOasis gives every resident and housing society a clear path from “that’s broken” to “it’s fixed”, and turns their complaints into evidence that holds departments to account.

Problem
Complaints reach the wrong department and nobody follows up, so they quietly die.
Solution
Automatic routing plus a follow-up, escalation and RTI engine with ready-made letters.
Customer
Free for residents. Paid by housing societies and RWAs that chase civic problems every month.
Model
₹999 per society per month. Federations and ward dashboards on custom pricing.
Edge
The only tool focused on follow-through, plus pooled fix-time data that nobody else collects.
Impact
SDG 11 and 16: safer streets, basic services that work, and more accountable local government.
